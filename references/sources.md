# Portable reference library

All required references are bundled in this repository. No RPW workspace, Discord history, Drive access or credentials are required.

## Approved rendering style

[style-approved.png](../assets/style-approved.png) is the approved rainy-tram rendering reference, selected on 2026-09-13. It controls chunky pixel clusters, stepped contours and low detail. Later examples do not replace it unless the user explicitly says so.

## Complete brand logo

[vinci-full-logo.png](../assets/vinci-full-logo.png) is the supplied transparent logo approved on 2026-10-01. Preserve VINCI WORLD and the entire INCUBATED BY renaiss line. Use it once at bottom right, approximately 26% visible canvas width; on 1672×941, the visible mark is approximately 435×172 with 14 px right/bottom margins. Exclude transparent outer padding when measuring. User instructions can override placement or omit the logo.

## Scope of subsequent corrections

September 2026 corrections require varied camera angles in batches, low-detail rendering, and preservation of expression during style-only edits. Card-pull scenes are optional and brief-dependent. A request to smile in one edit does not make every character permanently smile. Generated scenes do not establish official features, probabilities or rewards.

Bundled asset hashes and dimensions are in [asset-manifest.json](asset-manifest.json). Private operational logs and external asset dependencies are deliberately omitted from this portable edition.
